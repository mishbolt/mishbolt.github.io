About

I am a PhD student in mathematics at North Carolina State University. I previously studied mathematics and physics at Northern Arizona University and completed an M.S. in mathematics there.

My research background includes numerical modeling, computational topology, and combinatorial game theory. In my master's thesis I used persistent homology and machine learning to analyze parameter estimation for a nonlinear PDE. My earlier work involved numerical studies of a double pendulum. I have also coauthored a paper on isomorphism theorems for impartial games.

I am interested in research problems that combine mathematical analysis, algorithms, and scientific computing.
