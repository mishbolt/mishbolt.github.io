About

Education, research background, and technical experience.

I am a mathematics PhD student at North Carolina State University.

I studied mathematics and physics at Northern Arizona University, where I later completed an MS in mathematics. My background includes computational modeling, mathematical research, and university teaching.

For my master's thesis, I used persistent homology and machine learning to study parameter estimation from numerical solutions of a partial differential equation. Earlier, I studied nonlinear dynamics in a double-pendulum model. I have also coauthored research on the algebraic theory of impartial combinatorial games.

My current interests include scientific machine learning, PDEs, dynamical systems, and topology. I am interested in computational research and scientific software roles as well as academic collaboration.
