Isomorphism theorems for impartial combinatorial games

Journal article · Discrete Mathematics Letters · 2025

Overview

In joint work with Dana C. Ernst and Nándor Sieben, we studied algebraic structures associated with impartial combinatorial games. We use directed graphs, called optiongraphs, to encode positions and available moves.

Approach

Option-preserving maps provide a way to compare these graphs. The framework includes games with cycles or infinite play and supports the study of congruences and quotients.

Results

The paper develops analogues of classical algebraic isomorphism theorems in this setting and examines the game-theoretic information preserved by appropriate maps.

Publication

M. Baltushkin, D. C. Ernst, and N. Sieben. “Isomorphism theorems for impartial combinatorial games.” Discrete Mathematics Letters 16 (2025), 59–66.
