Isomorphism theorems for impartial combinatorial games

Joint work with Dana C. Ernst and Nándor Sieben

This work studies algebraic structures associated with impartial combinatorial games and their representations as directed graphs.

Framework

We use optiongraphs and option-preserving maps to describe games in a setting that permits cycles and infinite play. The framework provides a language for congruences and quotient constructions.

Results

The paper establishes analogues of the isomorphism theorems familiar from algebra and studies their consequences for optiongraphs. The approach also addresses game-theoretic quantities preserved under suitable maps.

Publication

M. Baltushkin, D. C. Ernst, and N. Sieben, “Isomorphism theorems for impartial combinatorial games,” Discrete Mathematics Letters 16 (2025), 59–66.
