Research

My interests include scientific machine learning and inverse problems, dynamical systems, topological data analysis, and algebraic combinatorial game theory.

Publications

M. Baltushkin, D. C. Ernst, and N. Sieben. “Isomorphism theorems for impartial combinatorial games.” Discrete Mathematics Letters 16 (2025), 59–66.

The paper studies optiongraphs and option-preserving maps, including congruences, quotient constructions, and corresponding isomorphism theorems.

Thesis

M. Baltushkin. Applications of Topological Data Analysis in Parameter Estimation for Partial Differential Equations. M.S. thesis, Northern Arizona University, 2023.

Advisor: Rachel Neville. Investigated persistent-homology representations of simulated PDE surfaces and evaluated them for parameter classification.
