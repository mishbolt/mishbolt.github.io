Research interests

Questions I keep returning to.

01

Scientific machine learning

Data-informed inference for dynamical systems and partial differential equations, with an interest in interpretability and mathematical structure.

02

Topology and geometry of data

Extracting global structure from complex datasets through persistent homology and topological signatures.

03

Algebraic combinatorial game theory

Optiongraphs, folding maps, and the mathematical structures underlying impartial games.

Research perspective

I am interested in mathematical methods that explain why algorithms work—and in computations that reveal structure worth studying mathematically.

Current position: PhD student in mathematics, North Carolina State University. Prior training: mathematics and physics, Northern Arizona University.
